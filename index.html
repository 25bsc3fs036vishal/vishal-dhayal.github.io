Who I Am

I am Vishal Dhayal, a postgraduate student pursuing an M.Sc. in Forensic Science at Vivekananda Global University, Jaipur. My academic journey is driven by curiosity, innovation, and a commitment to advancing forensic science through meaningful research and scientific exploration.

My work focuses on forensic biology, DNA analysis, forensic identification, emerging forensic technologies, scientific writing, and innovation. Alongside publishing research papers and authoring books, I strive to develop practical solutions that bridge the gap between academic research and real-world forensic applications.

I believe that science should not only answer today's questions but also inspire tomorrow's discoveries. My long-term vision is to contribute to forensic science through research, innovation, education, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

CENTRIGO

Portable Power for Precision

Project Overview

CENTRIGO is a portable, battery-powered forensic centrifuge developed to facilitate rapid on-site processing of biological samples during crime scene investigations. Designed with portability and efficiency in mind, it supports forensic professionals by enabling preliminary sample preparation before laboratory analysis, helping preserve evidence quality and reduce transportation delays.

Key Features

  • ✔ Portable and Compact Design
  • ✔ Battery Powered Operation
  • ✔ Four Sample Processing Capacity
  • ✔ Adjustable Speed Control
  • ✔ Safety Lid Lock Mechanism
  • ✔ Designed for Crime Scene & Field Investigations

Applications

  • Forensic Biology
  • Forensic Serology
  • Crime Scene Investigation
  • Biological Evidence Processing
  • DNA Sample Preparation
  • Academic Demonstration & Research

Innovation

CENTRIGO was developed to address the need for a portable centrifugation solution in forensic field investigations. The project combines portability, ease of use, and practical functionality to support faster preliminary processing of biological evidence outside conventional laboratory settings.

Current Status

This project is currently undergoing intellectual property protection. Detailed technical specifications, design architecture, and engineering documentation will be made publicly available after completion of the protection process.
